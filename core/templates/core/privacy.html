What we collect

When you order we collect your name, phone number, delivery address and, if you give one, your email. If you create an account we store your username and a hashed password — never the password itself.

Why we hold it

Only to take payment, deliver your order, and answer you if you get in touch. We do not sell your details or send marketing you did not ask for.

Who else sees it

Your phone number and the amount go to Bila, our payment provider, to charge you. Your email address goes to Resend, which delivers your receipt. That is all. We never see or store your mobile money PIN.

Cookies

One cookie, to remember what is in your bag and keep you logged in. There is no advertising or tracking cookie on this site.

Your choices

Ask us at {{ contact_email }} for a copy of what we hold, or for it to be deleted. We keep order records for accounting, so some details may have to stay.
